FLL Explore

Age: 6-10 years CAD $40/hr

Promotions: Please contact us for special offers and discounts

Course Overview

FIRST® LEGO® League Explore (formerly FLL Jr.) introduces young students to the exciting world of STEM through hands-on exploration. Teams work together to build and program a LEGO model that addresses a real-world challenge theme.

Real Classroom Experience

What Students Will Learn

  • Basic robotics and engineering concepts
  • Simple programming using LEGO WeDo or SPIKE Essential
  • Teamwork and collaboration skills
  • Problem-solving through hands-on building
  • Presentation and communication skills
  • Exploration of real-world themes and challenges

Course Structure

Program Format: Team-based learning experience

Prerequisites: None. Children join with no previous coding or robotics experience.

Platform: LEGO Education WeDo or SPIKE Essential, programmed with drag-and-drop icon blocks rather than typed code

Class Size: Maximum 6 students per class

Equipment: Usually 1 robotics kit per student for regular class work

Challenge / competition teams: For major challenge projects and competitions, students work in teams of 2–6

Materials: LEGO Education kits and materials provided for each student

Showcase: Teams present their projects at a local showcase event

FLL Challenge

Age: 9-14 years CAD $40/hr

Promotions: Please contact us for special offers and discounts

Course Overview

FIRST® LEGO® League Challenge is the most advanced level of FLL programs. Students design, build, and program autonomous robots to complete missions on a themed playing field. Teams also research and develop innovative solutions to real-world problems, then present their findings to judges.

Real Classroom Experience

What Students Will Learn

  • Advanced robotics programming and strategy
  • FLL Challenge competition rules and mission planning
  • Engineering design process and robot optimization
  • Research methodology and innovation project development
  • Team dynamics, leadership, and collaboration
  • Presentation skills and technical communication
  • Time management and competition preparation

Course Structure

Program Format: Competition-focused team training

Prerequisites: None required. Students are placed by age and supported from the basics; previous LEGO or coding experience is helpful but not necessary.

Platform: LEGO SPIKE Prime or MINDSTORMS EV3. Both are programmed with Scratch-based block coding in the LEGO Education app. SPIKE Prime also supports Python, so ask SunBot whether typed Python is part of the current Challenge program.

Class Size: Maximum 6 students per class

Equipment: Usually 1 robotics kit per student for regular class work

Challenge / competition teams: For major challenge projects and competitions, students work in teams of 2–6

Materials: Competition-grade LEGO SPIKE Prime or EV3 kits included for each student

Competition: Includes registration for local and regional FLL Challenge events

Components: Robot Game (robotics), Innovation Project (research), and Core Values (teamwork)

Choosing Between Programs

Which course suits a 6-year-old or a complete beginner?

FLL Explore. It is built for ages 6–10 and assumes no prior experience, starting with guided building and icon-based programming before introducing motors and sensors. A six-year-old with no exposure to LEGO robotics or coding can join directly.

Should my child start with coding or robotics first?

For most children under about 10, robotics first works better. A robot gives immediate physical feedback — the wheel turns or it does not — which makes abstract programming ideas like sequence, loops and conditions concrete. Children who already enjoy Scratch or typed code can move straight into FLL Challenge if they are 9 or older. The parent's guide compares robotics and coding classes in more detail.

What is the difference between FLL Explore and FLL Challenge?

FLL Explore is an introductory, showcase-based program for ages 6–10 using LEGO WeDo or SPIKE Essential, where students build a model and present it. FLL Challenge is a competitive program for ages 9–14 using SPIKE Prime or EV3, where students program an autonomous robot to complete missions on a themed field, research an innovation project, and are judged on robot performance, project and Core Values. See the FIRST LEGO League page for how the divisions work.

What programming languages or platforms will students learn?

Students program LEGO Education hardware rather than a general-purpose language. FLL Explore uses drag-and-drop icon blocks in WeDo or SPIKE Essential. FLL Challenge uses the Scratch-based block language in the LEGO Education SPIKE or EV3 Classroom app. SPIKE Prime additionally supports Python, so ask SunBot whether typed Python is currently part of the Challenge program.
