SunBot Robotics Programming STEAM Education offers hands-on robotics, coding and STEAM classes for children in southwest Edmonton. Students build, program and test robots while developing engineering, problem-solving, teamwork and presentation skills in small classes of up to six students.

About SunBot

SunBot is a STEAM education program for families in southwest Edmonton. Children learn robotics and programming through hands-on building, engineering challenges, teamwork and project-based learning.

Programs are organized primarily for ages 6–10 and ages 9–14, including FIRST® LEGO® League Explore and Challenge pathways. Classes are capped at six students, and children typically work in teams of two.

Looking for details parents often ask first? Read the parent robotics FAQ or the parent's guide to robotics classes for kids in Edmonton.

Why SunBot

Differences parents can verify

  • Small Classes

    Classes capped at six students so instructors can support building, coding and debugging more closely.

  • Collaborative Learning

    Typically two students work together as a team on a robot, practicing communication and shared problem solving.

  • Build + Code

    Students do not only learn programming concepts; they apply code to physical robots with motors and sensors.

  • Engineering Challenges

    Students test, debug and improve designs as part of hands-on robotics and engineering learning.

  • Communication

    Activities include teamwork and presentations so children practice explaining their ideas and designs.

  • Age Progression

    Content supports different ages and goals through Explore (ages 6–10) and Challenge (ages 9–14) pathways.
