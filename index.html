About SunBot

SunBot Robotics Programming STEAM Education offers hands-on robotics, coding and STEAM classes for children in southwest Edmonton. Students build, program and test robots while developing engineering, problem-solving, teamwork and presentation skills in small classes of up to six students.

Programs are organized for ages 6–10 (FLL Explore) and ages 9–14 (FLL Challenge), using LEGO robotics and LEGO Education kits such as LEGO SPIKE where applicable. Families comparing options can read the parent robotics FAQ or parent's guide to robotics classes in Edmonton.

Why SunBot

Differences parents can verify

  • Small Classes

    Classes capped at six students for closer guidance during building, coding and debugging.

  • Collaborative Learning

    In regular class work, each student usually has their own robotics kit. For major challenge projects and competitions, students work in teams of 2–6.

  • Build + Code

    Students apply programming to physical robots instead of only watching screen-based lessons.

  • Engineering Challenges

    Students test, debug and improve designs through hands-on problem solving.

  • Communication

    Activities include teamwork and presentations so children practice explaining their work.

  • Age Pathways

    Clear tracks for ages 6–10 and 9–14, from beginner exploration to FLL Challenge preparation.

Robotics Classes in Southwest Edmonton

SunBot serves families in southwest Edmonton, Alberta, looking for robotics classes for kids, LEGO robotics / LEGO SPIKE learning, and STEAM activities that combine coding with building.

Classes run in the Terwillegar area of southwest Edmonton, near Terwillegar Community Recreation Centre. The recreation centre is a nearby landmark for orientation only; classes are not held there. Use the contact page to confirm the exact class location before travelling.
