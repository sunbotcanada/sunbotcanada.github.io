About SunBot

SunBot Robotics Programming STEAM Education offers hands-on robotics and coding classes for children in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. Classes run in the Terwillegar area of southwest Edmonton, serving families from Riverbend, Windermere, Heritage Valley and nearby communities. Students build, program and test robots in small classes of up to six students, developing engineering, problem-solving, teamwork and presentation skills.

Programs are organized for ages 6–10 (FLL Explore) and ages 9–14 (FLL Challenge), using LEGO robotics and LEGO Education kits such as LEGO SPIKE where applicable. Families comparing options can read the parent robotics FAQ, the parent's guide to robotics classes in Edmonton, or the course comparison table. New families can also learn about SunBot and its instructor.

Quick Answers for Parents

What is SunBot?

SunBot is a robotics and coding education program for children in Edmonton, Alberta. Students build physical robots with LEGO Education kits and program them to complete tasks, rather than only following screen-based coding lessons.

Where is SunBot located, and which parts of Edmonton does it serve?

Classes run in the Terwillegar area of southwest Edmonton. Families attend from Riverbend, Terwillegar, Windermere, Keswick, Rutherford, Heritage Valley and other nearby communities. The full list of communities served is on the contact page, which is also where you can confirm the exact class location before travelling.

What robotics and coding programs are available?

Two programs, both based on FIRST LEGO League: FLL Explore for ages 6–10, which introduces robotics through guided building and simple programming, and FLL Challenge for ages 9–14, which covers autonomous robot missions, an innovation project and competition preparation.

What ages does SunBot teach, and are beginners welcome?

SunBot teaches children aged 6 to 14, split into the 6–10 and 9–14 age groups. No previous coding or robotics experience is required. Beginners start with guided building and simple programs, then progress to sensors, motors and debugging.

How much do classes cost?

Course pages list CAD $40 per hour. Promotions and current offers are handled by inquiry, so contact SunBot for pricing on a specific program.

Does SunBot offer trial classes or camps?

No trial-class policy or camp schedule is published on this website. Parents who want to try a class first, or who are asking about school-break programming, should email SunBot and ask what is currently available for their child's age group.

How do parents ask about enrollment?

Email sunbotcanada@gmail.com, use the contact form, or message on WeChat (ID sunbotcanada) or Instagram. Mention your child's age and any prior experience so SunBot can recommend an age group.

Why SunBot

Differences parents can verify

  • Small Classes

    Classes capped at six students for closer guidance during building, coding and debugging.

  • Collaborative Learning

    In regular class work, each student usually has their own robotics kit. For major challenge projects and competitions, students work in teams of 2–6.

  • Build + Code

    Students apply programming to physical robots instead of only watching screen-based lessons.

  • Engineering Challenges

    Students test, debug and improve designs through hands-on problem solving.

  • Communication

    Activities include teamwork and presentations so children practice explaining their work.

  • Age Pathways

    Clear tracks for ages 6–10 and 9–14, from beginner exploration to FLL Challenge preparation.
