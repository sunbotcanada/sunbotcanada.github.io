Our Mission

SunBot Robotics Programming STEAM Education provides hands-on robotics, coding and STEAM learning for children in southwest Edmonton. Students build, program and test robots while developing problem-solving, teamwork and presentation skills.

Our mission is to:

  • Teach robotics and programming through hands-on projects
  • Support engineering thinking, debugging and iteration
  • Build confidence through teamwork and presentations
  • Offer small-group learning for children ages 6–10 and 9–14

Our Vision

We want children in southwest Edmonton to have access to practical STEAM learning where they can build, code, test and share their work.

SunBot focuses on clear age pathways, small classes, and FIRST® LEGO® League–inspired programs so families can choose exploration or competition-oriented learning with transparent program details.

Why SunBot

Differences parents can verify

  • Small Classes

    Classes capped at six students for closer guidance during building, coding and debugging.

  • Collaborative Learning

    In regular class work, each student usually has their own robotics kit. For major challenge projects and competitions, students work in teams of 2–6 and practice shared problem solving.

  • Build + Code

    Children apply programming to physical robots instead of only completing screen-based exercises.

  • FLL Pathways

    Programs align with FLL Explore (ages 6–10) and FLL Challenge (ages 9–14) learning goals.
